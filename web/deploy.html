Deploy HandoffRouter

Signs locally in your browser. Your key never leaves this page — there is no server, no upload, no analytics.

Why this page exists. The sandbox can't hold a key safely, and the faucet rate-limited you. So deployment happens in your browser, with your wallet, using the exact bytecode from the tested build.
